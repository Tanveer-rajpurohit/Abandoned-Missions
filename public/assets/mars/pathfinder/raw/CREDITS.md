## Key facts (from the same page)

- Launch 4 Dec 1996 (UTC), Delta II 7925, Cape Canaveral
- Landed 4 Jul 1997, Ares Vallis (ancient flood plain)
- Airbags inflated ~100 m up; ~15 bounces, up to 15 m high; stopped ~1 km from first impact after 2.5 min
- Sojourner: 10.6 kg, 30 cm tall, rolled down a 20° ramp on sol 2 (night of 5 Jul)
- Lander renamed Carl Sagan Memorial Station
- Last contact 27 Sep 1997
- Returned 16,500+ lander images, 550 rover images, 15+ chemical analyses, weather data
- Findings: rounded pebbles → past running water; magnetic dust; dust devils; morning water-ice clouds
